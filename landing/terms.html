Using KickFeed

KickFeed lets you follow football, talk with other fans, and make opinion picks. The service is provided as available. Live scores can be delayed or missing when the data source is down.

Not gambling

Score picks and Man of the Match votes are fan opinions for a leaderboard. KickFeed is not a betting or gambling product. There are no stakes and no payouts.

Fantasy mini-leagues

Private mini-leagues are free to play with friends. There are no stakes, entry fees, or payouts. KickFeed is not a betting or gambling product. A goal in a covered match is the only score in this version.

Your content

You are responsible for what you post and send. Do not harass other fans. Blocking and reporting hide content on your account. KickFeed is not a full moderation service.

Scores, lineups, and TV

When a lineup, statistic, or live score is missing, KickFeed shows an empty state. It does not invent a lineup, possession, shots, or expected goals. TV listings are editorial and can be wrong.

Accounts

Keep your password to yourself. Demo profiles are samples on this device, not a private identity. These terms are the copy shipped in this app build. The store listing will link the same public page.
