## Full description

KickFeed is a football feed and a scores board in one app.

Follow the match, talk with other fans, and pick a score or a Man of the Match. Those picks are opinions for a fan leaderboard. They are not bets. There are no stakes and no payouts.

Live scores cover the Premier League, the EFL Championship, Slovakia Niké Liga, and La Liga. Other competitions in the app are sample data and are labeled mock. TV listings are editorial notes, not a licensed guide.

Sign in with email. Report or block another fan from a post, a profile, or a message. Match alerts are off until you turn them on.
